// HOW TO USE THE CHAMBER
🟠
Each dot = one MP
The chamber shows 543 seats arranged in 6 blocks of 11 rows — exactly as the real Lok Sabha. Each coloured dot is one elected MP, coloured by their party.
⭕
Gold ring = Cabinet minister
Ministers have a gold outer ring around their seat dot. PM Modi, Amit Shah, Rajnath Singh and other cabinet members are on the right side, front rows.
👆
Hover to identify
Move your cursor over any dot to see the MP's name, party, constituency and case count instantly. MPs with criminal cases show a red badge.
🗂
Click to open dossier
Click any seat to open the GTA-style dossier card — real IPC sections, asset declarations, wanted-level stars, and party info from ADR affidavit data.
⬅ ➡
Left vs right side
Right side of the Speaker = ruling NDA coalition. Left side = INDIA opposition. Front rows = senior leaders. This mirrors the actual chamber.
✕
Close & navigate
Press Esc or click ✕ to close the dossier panel. The chamber rescales automatically. Works on any screen size — try resizing the window.
